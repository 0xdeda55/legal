0xdeda55-Oauth

0xdeda55-Oauth is a personal content production and publishing tool. It connects to its operator's own social media accounts — YouTube, TikTok and Instagram — through each platform's official OAuth flow, so that videos the operator produces can be uploaded, scheduled and maintained from one place.

Who this application is for

This is a single-operator tool. It is not offered as a public service and has no user sign-up. The only accounts it ever connects to are those of its operator, who authorizes each connection personally through the platform's own consent screen.

What it does

  • Produces and reviews long-form and short-form video content.
  • Uploads finished videos to the operator's own channels, with titles, descriptions and thumbnails.
  • Maintains published videos — for example configuring thumbnail A/B tests and end screens.
  • Reads back the operator's own channel statistics to inform future content.

Which permissions it requests, and why

  • youtube.readonly — list the operator's own videos and their statistics.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read the operator's own audience and watch-time data.
  • youtube.upload — upload videos and set thumbnails on the operator's own channels.
  • youtube.force-ssl — post the operator's own comments and manage their own videos.

The application only ever acts on the operator's own channels, and only on actions the operator explicitly starts. It does not act on behalf of any third party.

Data handling

The application runs locally on the operator's own machine. Content and OAuth tokens are stored on that machine and are not sold, shared or transferred to anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for details, and the Terms of Service for the terms of use.
